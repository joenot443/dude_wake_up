# Audio Pendulum

A pendulum-wave kinetic sculpture in a dark gallery. A row of polished spheres hangs on
fine wires from a beam; each pendulum is a little shorter and faster than the last, so the
row runs through travelling waves, snakes, apparent chaos and two-row splits before lining
up perfectly again once a cycle (about a minute). Ray traced with a glossy floor, soft
shadows, reflections of the studio lights and the neighbouring spheres, light haze and
depth of field.

**Audio mapping**
- **Kick** — a synchronised impulse: every pendulum's swing swells, then relaxes, and the key light lifts. Kicks are detected relative to the track's own running bass level, so heavily compressed masters still read.
- **Bass** — overall swing amplitude.
- **Mids** — the pattern's clock speed, a slow camera orbit and the warmth of the light.
- **Highs** — sparkle: the studio lights flare in the spheres and dust glints in the haze.

**Events** — the lighting crossfades every ~40 s between a warm brass gallery, a cold blue
night and a single hard theatre spotlight; the camera eases between a low three-quarter
view, a view down the row (where the waves read best) and a wide front view. The haze
swells as the row re-aligns. With no audio the pendulums keep swinging gently.

**Parameters**
- **Swing** — swing amplitude (and kick strength).
- **Kick Punch** — how hard each kick pushes the row.
- **Pendulums** — number of pendulums in the row.
- **Camera Drift** — amount of slow camera orbit.
- **Key Light** — key light intensity (floor pool, haze, highlights).
- **Depth Of Field** — focus blur away from the row's centre.
- **Haze** — light shafts and dust in the air.
- **Hue Shift** — rotates the palette.
- **Audio Sensitivity** — gain on the band levels and the kick detector.
- **Brightness** — exposure into the tone-mapping.

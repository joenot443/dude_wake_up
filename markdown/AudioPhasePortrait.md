# Audio Phase Portrait

The sound drawn as light on a phosphor oscilloscope. A beam traces a slowly morphing
3D harmonograph whose form follows the spectrum (two pens, joined by fine string-art
rulings), over a faint graticule. Feedback is the phosphor: fresh strokes burn hot and
cool into an afterglow and then a deep tone, always fading fully to black. Inside it
rides a genuine phase portrait of the music — one scope-triggered period of the raw
waveform plotted against delayed copies of itself — which locks into a clean closed
loop on sustained notes and scatters on noise and drums.

**Audio mapping**
- **Spectrum** — the figure's proportions (slow, so it reads as one object).
- **Pitch / tonality** — the phase-portrait loop: its timing comes from autocorrelation pitch tracking and it appears only when the sound is periodic and the loop closes.
- **Kick** — a flash of the figure, a beam surge and a strobe redraw of the whole closed shape.
- **Raw waveform** — a fine shiver along the beam.

**Events** — every 20 s the harmonograph ratios morph into a new figure and the
phosphor colour changes (P7 green, amber, teal); some epochs dive the camera into the
figure, and every couple of minutes a "long exposure" passage lets the trace build
into a dense time-exposure sculpture before releasing.

**Parameters**
- **Persistence** — phosphor decay.
- **Beam Intensity** — brightness of the live beam.
- **Rulings** — the string-art rulings between figure and echo shell.
- **Phase Portrait** — strength of the raw-waveform loop.
- **Kick Punch** — kick flash / surge / strobe amount.
- **Figure Size**, **Beam Speed**, **Wave Jitter** — figure scale, tracing speed, and beam shiver.
- **Hue Shift** — rotates the palette.
- **Audio Sensitivity** — spectrum gain.
- **Brightness** — exposure into the soft tone-mapping.

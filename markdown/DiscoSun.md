# DiscoSun

A raymarched corkscrew light tunnel with a warm IQ cosine palette, a pulsing
shimmer/strobe interference layer, and a soft vignette. Ported from "DiscoSun" by
@WorkingClassHacker (based on "Abstract Shine" by @Frostbyte), CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.

**Parameters**
- **Speed** — overall animation speed (forward motion + strobe rate).
- **Twist** — how tightly the tunnel corkscrews.
- **Shimmer** — strength of the high-frequency strobe/interference layer. Modulate to the beat for a disco flash.
- **Brightness** — exposure of the accumulated glow.

All four are audio-reactive via their oscillators.

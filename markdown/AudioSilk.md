# Audio Silk

Draped shot silk lit like a studio product shot. Long hanging folds (tapered turbulence
stretched lengthwise) carry fine wrinkles and pinched creases; a warm key light orbits
slowly against a cool rim light, so thin anisotropic (Kajiya-Kay) sheen lines ride each
fold flank and glide across the cloth. Highlights keep colour from the opposite dye,
like real shot silk. Three restrained dye palettes — ink/teal/plum with gold,
oxblood/rose with champagne, midnight/sapphire with silver — bleed into each other
along a ragged front every ~16 s.

**Audio mapping**
- **Kick** — a gust of light sweeps across the sheen, with a highlight-only contrast punch and a gentle billow through the folds.
- **Spectrum** — woven into the cloth: neighbouring folds carry neighbouring bands, and a thin seam on each fold's shoulder glows with its band.
- **Mids** — sheen intensity.
- **Highs** — rare fibre-aligned glints on the highlights.

**Parameters**
- **Speed** — animation rate (accumulated phase, so changing it eases rather than jumps).
- **Scale** — zoom of the fabric (higher = more, smaller folds).
- **Ripple** — fold amplitude of the drape.
- **Sheen** — strength of the anisotropic highlights.
- **Gust** — how strongly kicks sweep light and billow the cloth.
- **Hue Shift** — rotates the palette.
- **Audio Sensitivity** — gain on the incoming spectrum.
- **Brightness** — exposure into the soft tone-mapping.

With no audio it falls back to a dimmed, slowly drifting drape.

# Ferro

A flowing **ferrofluid / cosmic ink smoke** source. A cheap aperiodic dot-noise field is
domain-warped twice into billowing ink, then a third ridged octave carves fine filament
hair into it. Colored with a disciplined magenta↔teal sheen (no muddy amber), with deep
graded voids that read as a well with mass, a wet liquid-metal specular on the crests, and
a cinematic vignette.

## Parameters

- **Speed** — flow rate. Integrated as accumulated phase, so changing it eases the motion
  instead of warping the whole field.
- **Scale** — zoom of the smoke (smaller = larger billows).
- **Warp** — domain-warp strength; how turbulent / folded the billows are.
- **Detail** — strength of the ridged filament hair carved into the density.
- **Hue Shift** — rotates the palette through the magenta↔teal arc.
- **Saturation** — global color vividness (turn up for neon, down for smoky/painterly).
- **Brightness** — exposure.

All parameters are oscillator-bound and audio-reactive.

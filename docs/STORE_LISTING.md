## Store listing (English)

**Product name**: Nottawa

**Short description** (shown in search results, up to ~100 characters):

> Real-time audio-reactive visuals for live shows: connect sources and effects, play them to the music.

**Description**:

> Nottawa turns music into live visuals. Build a scene by connecting
> sources (shaders, video, images, text, your webcam) to effects in a node
> graph, and watch it react to the sound in real time: tempo, beats, bass,
> mids and highs drive any parameter you choose.
>
> Made for VJs, DJs, musicians and anyone putting on a show:
>
> - Over 170 sources and effects, from feedback and kaleidoscopes to
>   audio-reactive waveforms and spectrum visualisers.
> - Beat detection that follows the music, with tap tempo and Ableton Link.
> - Map parameters to audio, oscillators or a MIDI controller.
> - Send the output full screen to a projector or second display.
> - Save scenes as strands and workspaces; share strands with the
>   community and try other people's.
> - Your own shaders: edit the GLSL of any effect and see it live.
>
> Nottawa is free and open source (GPLv3).

## Privacy policy (to host at nottawa.app/privacy)

> **Nottawa privacy policy**
>
> Last updated: <date>
>
> Nottawa is a desktop app for live visuals. It runs on your computer and
> keeps your work on your computer. This policy explains the few things it
> sends over the internet.
>
> **What Nottawa sends**
>
> - **Community strands.** When you open the Community page, Nottawa asks
>   nottawa.app for the list of shared strands. Votes you cast are sent with
>   a random identifier created on your computer the first time you vote, so
>   one person counts as one vote. It isn't linked to your name, account or
>   device.
> - **Sharing a strand.** If you choose to share a strand, Nottawa uploads
>   it with the name and author name you enter and a preview image of it.
>   Shared strands are public: anyone can see and download them.
> - **Library.** When you browse or download from the Library, Nottawa
>   requests the list and the files you choose from nottawa.app and its file
>   storage provider.
> - **Updates** (copies not installed from the Microsoft Store). Once a day
>   Nottawa reads a small file at nottawa.app to see if a newer version is
>   out. You can turn this off in Settings > Updates.
>
> These requests, like any web request, reach our server with your IP
> address; our hosting provider may keep it in server logs for security and
> troubleshooting for a limited time.
>
> **What Nottawa doesn't do**
>
> - No accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking.
> - Camera and microphone input is processed on your computer only and is
>   never recorded or sent anywhere, unless you record or capture the output
>   yourself (saved to a folder you choose).
> - Your projects, settings, workspaces and media stay on your computer.
>
> **Removing something you shared**
>
> Email joe@nottawa.app with the strand's link and we'll remove it.
>
> **Children**
>
> Nottawa doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.
>
> **Changes**
>
> If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
> date.
>
> **Contact**
>
> Joe Crozier, joe@nottawa.app
